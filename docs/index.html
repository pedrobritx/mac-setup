A small handoff to your Mac.

The public website makes your plan. The local companion installs it and shows real progress.

  1. 1

    Keep your downloaded plan

    Save mac-setup-plan.json somewhere you can find it.

  2. 2

    Open the companion from Terminal

    Open Terminal (⌘ Space, then “Terminal”), paste this command and press Return. Apple’s Command Line Tools may need a first-time installation.

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pedrobritx/mac-setup/main/bootstrap.sh | bash -s -- --web

    You can read the launcher first. Already have a clone? Run ./web.sh.

  3. 3

    Import, review, install

    The companion opens in your browser. Choose “Import plan”, select your JSON file, review your choices and confirm. Keep Terminal open for password prompts.

This website cannot inspect or change your Mac. Live progress appears after you launch the local companion.
